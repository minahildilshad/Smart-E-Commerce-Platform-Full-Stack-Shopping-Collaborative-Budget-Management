# Orbit 3D catalog attribution

All product models and their supplied preview images are obtained from KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets:
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets

These files are reused as a licensed sample ecommerce catalog. Orbit display names, sizes, sample PKR prices, discounts, and quantities are added for the store demonstration. They are not offers from the original asset contributors or endorsements by those organizations. The GLB geometry and textures are supplied without visual modification. The Studio Sneaker uses a matching single-shoe preview supplied with the earlier Orbit version; its original source model is unchanged. The other previews are source repository renders, not new product photographs. Lighting, camera framing, and default materials can differ between the preview and interactive rendering while representing the same product.
